# What Is Google Business Manager?

The term **Google Business Manager** commonly refers to Google's business tools, especially **Google Business Profile**, Google Ads, Google Analytics, and related account-management features. Google Business Profile allows an organization to manage how it appears on Google Search and Google Maps.

## Main Features

- Business name, address, phone number, website, and opening hours.
- Business category, products, services, photos, and posts.
- Customer reviews and responses.
- Messages, calls, directions, and website visits.
- User roles and permissions for people managing the account.
- Connections with advertising and analytics tools.

## How to Create and Manage a Business Profile

1. Sign in with a Google account.
2. Add or claim the business profile.
3. Enter accurate business information.
4. Verify the business using the available verification method.
5. Add photos, services, products, and a website link.
6. Monitor and respond to reviews and customer questions.
7. Check performance insights regularly.

## Data Analytics Uses

Business insights can show searches, views, calls, website clicks, direction requests, and customer interactions. An analyst can compare performance by month, location, service, or campaign and calculate metrics such as click-through rate and cost per lead.

For example, a local restaurant can compare direction requests and phone calls before and after a promotion. This helps determine whether the promotion generated useful customer activity.

## Good Practices

- Keep business information accurate and consistent.
- Use high-quality, genuine images.
- Respond professionally to reviews.
- Give account access only to trusted users.
- Do not publish private customer information.
- Compare profile data with website analytics and sales data.

## Conclusion

Google's business tools help organizations manage their online presence and measure customer interactions. Their insights are useful for local marketing, customer behavior analysis, and business decision-making.
